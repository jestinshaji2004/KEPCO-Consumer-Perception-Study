# KEPCO Consumer Perception Study

## 📌 Project Overview

This project, titled **“A Study on Consumer Perception of the Product Quality of KEPCO Poultry Products in Pettah, Thiruvananthapuram,”** examines consumer opinions and perceptions regarding the quality of poultry products offered by KEPCO.

The study focuses on understanding customers' quality perceptions, the factors influencing their opinions, satisfaction with product quality, and their views on customer service.

## 🎯 Objectives of the Study

- To study consumer perception of the quality of KEPCO poultry products.
- To identify the factors influencing consumers' opinions about product quality.
- To assess customer satisfaction with the quality of KEPCO poultry products.
- To evaluate consumer perceptions of KEPCO's customer service quality.

## 📍 Scope of the Study

The study focuses on consumers of KEPCO poultry products in Pettah, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala.

The products covered include:
- Chicken
- Turkey
- Duck
- Quail

The study examines product quality perception, customer satisfaction, factors affecting consumer opinions, and customer service.

## 🔍 Research Methodology

The project follows a consumer-focused research approach to understand opinions and satisfaction regarding KEPCO poultry products.

The study uses a questionnaire-based approach to collect consumer responses.

**Key areas of investigation:**
- Perceived product quality
- Factors influencing purchase and quality perception
- Customer satisfaction
- Customer service quality

## 📊 Key Areas of Analysis

- **Product Quality Perception:** Understanding how consumers evaluate the quality of KEPCO poultry products.
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Examining consumer satisfaction with product quality.
- **Influencing Factors:** Identifying factors that shape consumer opinions.
- **Customer Service:** Understanding consumer perceptions of the service provided.

## 💡 Purpose of the Study

The study aims to provide insights into consumer expectations and perceptions that may help identify areas for improvement in product quality and customer service.

## 🎓 Project Information

- **Project Type:** College Academic Project
- **Organization Studied:** KEPCO
- **Study Location:** Pettah, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala
- **Research Area:** Marketing and Consumer Behaviour
- **Project Topic:** Consumer Perception of Product Quality
